# 2-minute demo

1. **Overview** (0:00) — the six steps, each with progress and one next action, computed from the data. The sample's weak spot is the profile (38/100).
2. **1 Research** (0:20) — the language bank: “content gets likes but no leads” was said by 4 of 10 buyers. Switch the breakdown to *Hook type* to see which hooks score highest for competitors.
3. **2 Profile** (0:45) — click the suggested headline, then type an About section that names the buyer's problem, your approach, proof and “book a call”. The audit climbs as you type.
4. **3–4 Content** (1:05) — press *Use* on an idea prompt; type a hook starting “Stop…” and watch the checks classify it as contrarian and flag the missing closing question. Change “What the post shows” to *A result* — it recommends a highlighted screenshot.
5. **5 Engage** (1:30) — log a 15-minute comment; the streak and chart update.
6. **6 Analytics** (1:40) — Proof posts: *Double down*; Expertise: *Cut back*; Industry take: *Need more posts*.
7. **Data** (1:55) — create a client workspace and import `sample-data/buyers-example.csv`.
